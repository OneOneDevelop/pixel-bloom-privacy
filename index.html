Pixel Bloom – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [release date]

Pixel Bloom is a puzzle game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what data is handled when you play. Contact: oneone.developer@gmail.com

What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, settings and saved puzzles are stored only on your device. You do not need to create an account to play.

Photos you choose for “Photo puzzle” are processed on your device to build a level. The photo is not uploaded. If you send a puzzle to a friend, only the small pixel picture and its puzzle data are included in the code you share.

Leaderboards and backup

To run leaderboards and keep a backup of your progress, the game signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase and stores, under a random identifier: a display name (randomly generated unless you set your own, see below), your country, your scores, and a copy of your game progress and settings (including your language and country). Your country is the one you pick in Settings; until you pick one, the game guesses it from your device's time zone or region setting, not from your location. Your display name, avatar, country and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards, in the weekend challenge and in your team. We do not collect your precise location.

Cheers, gift links and teams store the same display name and country, which team members and the people you share links with can see. Messages between players are always chosen from a fixed list in the game; you cannot type messages to other players.

Optional sign-in

You can choose to save your progress with an Apple account on iOS, or a Google account on Android, under Settings → Save your progress. Firebase then receives the name and email address of that account, which are used only to sign you in and restore your progress on another device. They are never shown to other players. Playing without signing in is always possible.

Your profile

After you save your progress you can fill in a profile, all of it optional:

  • Display name. Shown to other players instead of the generated name. Names are checked against a list of offensive words before they are saved. Any player can report a name they find offensive; we store which account reported which name, and a name reported by several players is replaced by “Pixel Player” for everyone.
  • Avatar. One of the game's own characters, shown next to your name. You cannot upload pictures.
  • Year of birth. Stored only in your private backup and never shown to anyone. It is used to keep ads suitable for your age: if it shows you are under 13, the game uses its generated name for you and asks Google to treat ad requests as directed at children.

You can change or clear your profile at any time in the game, and deleting your account removes it.

Deleting your account and data

In the game, open Settings → Delete my account and data. This removes your scores, your cloud backup and your account from our server, and then your progress on the device. If you no longer have the game installed, email us from the address above. Full details: how to delete your Pixel Bloom account.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, in order to show, limit and measure advertising. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.

On iOS, tracking across other companies’ apps and websites happens only if you allow it in the system prompt. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

Pixel Bloom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If a player gives a year of birth that shows they are under 13, the game does not let them set a display name of their own and requests child-directed ads only. A parent who believes their child has given us personal data can ask us to delete it at the address above.

Your rights

You can delete your data at any time as described above; uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device. For questions about data handled by Google’s advertising services, or to exercise rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us at the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
