Pixel Bloom – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Pixel Bloom is a puzzle game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what data is handled when you play. Contact: oneone.developer@gmail.com

What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, settings and saved puzzles are stored only on your device. You do not need to create an account to play.

Photos you choose for “Photo puzzle” are processed on your device to build a level. The photo is not uploaded. If you send a puzzle to a friend, only the small pixel picture and its puzzle data are included in the code you share.

Leaderboards and backup

To run leaderboards and keep a backup of your progress, the game signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase and stores, under a random identifier: a randomly generated display name, your country (taken from your device's region setting, not your location), your scores, and a copy of your game progress. Your display name, country and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards. We do not collect your real name, email address or precise location. To have this data deleted, contact us at the address above.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, in order to show, limit and measure advertising. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.

On iOS, tracking across other companies’ apps and websites happens only if you allow it in the system prompt. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

Pixel Bloom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because game data is kept on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the game. For questions about data handled by Google’s advertising services, or to exercise rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us at the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
